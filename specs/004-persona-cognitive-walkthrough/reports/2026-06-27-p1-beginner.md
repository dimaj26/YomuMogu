```

   └─"Сохранить и начать" (nothing marked — she knows nothing)──> deck seeded (500)
```

## 3. Per-screen, in-character observations

### 3.1 `/` landing (pre-diagnostic)

- **Q1 Clarity**: Mostly clear. The H1 «Превратите ваши слова в живую речь!» + the
  one-line intro tells me it's Japanese practice with an AI tutor and a built-in
  deck. The big mascot and "0 / 20 XP / Уровень погружения 0" are friendly. ✅
- **Q2 Next step**: The single bright CTA «Пройти диагностику (5 мин)» tells me
  where to click. ✅ **But** the mascot bubble says *«Перейди в раздел практики и
  выбери тему!»* while there is **no "Практика" link** in the nav (only Настройки /
  Профиль / Справка). The mascot points me somewhere that isn't there. ⚠️
- **Q3 Missing function**: As a total newcomer I'd want a 1-line "what will happen"
  or a tiny "how it works" — the marketing line is nice but I don't know what
  "диагностика" will do to me before clicking. No demo/preview.
- **Q4 Convenience**: Clean, one obvious action. The Kumiko grid is pretty but I
  don't understand it yet (it correctly says "колода ещё не инициализирована" —
  good, it doesn't lie). Convenient enough. ✅
- **Outcome**: advances. Console: 1 warning (CSS preload, benign). Network: none
  (static only). Screenshot: [p1-01-home.png](screenshots/p1-01-home.png).

### 3.2 Diagnostic modal («Диагностика знаний»)

- **Q1 Clarity**: The instruction «Отметьте слова, которые вы уже хорошо знаете»
  is clear. Tabs N5 / N4 / «Разговорный слой» and categories (Существительные,
  Глаголы…). ✅
- **Q2 Next step**: Clear — mark what I know, then «Сохранить и начать». As a **zero
  beginner I mark nothing** and just save. That works, but it feels slightly odd to
  be shown 500 words to "assess" when I know none — a true beginner might wonder
  "am I supposed to know these?" A "I'm a complete beginner, skip" shortcut would
  reassure me. ⚠️ (learning-logic: the diagnostic assumes some prior knowledge to
  triage; the zero case is a no-op the user must intuit.)
- **Q3 Missing function**: A "select none / I'm brand new" express path; an
  explanation of what "Разговорный слой" means.
- **Q4 Convenience**: Long scroll of words across 3 tabs × 4 categories — fine to
  skip, potentially heavy if I *did* try to triage. Modal is dismissible. ✅
- **Outcome**: advances (saving seeds the 500-word deck). Console: 2 warnings.
  Screenshot: [p1-02-diagnostic.png](screenshots/p1-02-diagnostic.png).

### 3.3 `/` landing (post-diagnostic)

- **Q1 Clarity**: The page **adapted** — H1 is now «Начнём с разминки!» with a
  «Начать разминку» button, and the Kumiko description switched to the real
  500-word text. I clearly progressed. ✅ (Nice adaptive hub.)
- **Q2 Next step**: Very clear — one button, «Начать разминку». ✅
- **Q3 Missing function**: The mascot bubble **still** says «Перейди в раздел
  практики» (stale vs the new CTA). Minor.
- **Q4 Convenience**: Good. Screenshot:
  [p1-03-home-postdiag.png](screenshots/p1-03-home-postdiag.png). Console: 3×
  the same benign CSS-preload warning (it multiplies per render).
- **Outcome**: advances.

### 3.4 `/practice`

- **Q1 Clarity**: Title «Практика диалога», a warning banner «Пока в работе 10
  слов — для диалога это немного…», «Новые слова на сегодня 0 из 10 / Всего
  неизученных 500», warm-up + «➕ Добавить +10». A lot at once, but readable. ✅/⚠️
- **Q2 Next step**: «🎯 Начать разминку» is the obvious action. ✅ But there are
  many competing panels (Активное повторение, Карта слов/грамматики/медиа, Баланс
  обучения, Квесты, Интервальные повторения) — as a beginner I don't know which
  matter yet. Mild overload.
- **Q3 Missing function**: «Активное повторение слов: Все активные слова повторены!
  Отличная работа.» — I have **never** reviewed anything, so "all reviewed, great
  job!" is **misleading praise** on day one. (learning-logic / copy.) ⚠️
- **Q4 Convenience**: The page is dense for a first-timer; the most important action
  (warm-up) competes with 6+ secondary cards. The chat panel says **«Разговорная
  практика заблокирована — нужно ≥5 слов в изучении»** with «Перейти к изучению» —
  this is a *clear, well-explained* gate (good), but it also means the headline
  feature (AI chat) is **not available on day one**. (learning-logic: see verdict.)
- **Outcome**: advances. Console: 4× benign CSS-preload warning. Network: none
  observed. Screenshot: [p1-04-practice.png](screenshots/p1-04-practice.png).

## 5. Learning-journey verdict (in character)

"Honestly, the **start was good**: one button took me into a diagnostic, and after
I saved, the home page *changed* to tell me to do a warm-up — I felt guided. The
**warm-up itself was the best part** — one word, its reading, meaning, and audio;
that's how I want to learn. But two things confused me as a newcomer: (1) the app
kept nudging me toward **‘разговорная практика с ИИ’** — the reason I came — yet
**chat is locked** until I've studied ≥5 words, and nothing lets me even *peek* at
it; and (2) the practice page is **busy** — six side-panels (balance, quests, FSRS
theory, word/grammar/media maps) compete with the one thing I should do first.
The learning order is *mostly* logical (diagnose → warm-up → quiz → unlock chat),
but it's **implicit** — I pieced it together, the app never said ‘here's your path:
1-2-3’. And being told ‘all words reviewed, great job!’ when I'd done nothing made
me distrust the praise."

- Gemini was available but P1 never reached an AI call (chat gated). MeCab/Anki down
