> Persona-driven cognitive walkthrough, feature 004. Persona in

## 1. Persona header

- **Persona**: P3 «Мария» — advanced learner (~N2), efficiency-driven power user.
  Wants to clear review load fast, find advanced/immersion practice, and trust the
  app scales to thousands of words.
- **profileId**: `p3_master` — **state: full seed (synthetic)**.
  - *Seeded*: 4000 words from the app's JLPT **N3+N2+N1** vocabulary, distributed
    across FSRS bands (~2500 mature / ~700 review / ~450 learning / ~250 new / ~100
    lapsed-ish) → **900 due now**. Glosses are **synthetic** (JLPT source gives
    word+reading; `translation` was filled from the reading) — disclosed.
  - *Important limitation*: the app **reconciles** the local deck
    (`__local_starter__`) against the canonical 500-word starter deck on
    `/practice` load and **purged** the synthetic 4000 mid-session (see §6). Valid
    at-scale observations below come from the **initial seeded state** (home +
    practice, screenshots p3-01/p3-02) before the purge.
- **Run**: 2026-06-27, `http://localhost:3000`, Playwright MCP headless.
- **Service availability**: Gemini **up** but session-generation **stalled** at
  scale (see §3.3); MeCab `:8000` **down**; AnkiConnect **down**.

## 3. Per-screen, in-character observations

### 3.1 `/` home (at scale)

- **Q1 Clarity**: «С возвращением!» + «900 слов(а) к повторению»; the Kumiko grid is
  now fully colored «Усвоенные, Стабильность 74–140дн». The scale is visible. ✅
- **Q2 Next step**: One clear button («Продолжить обучение»). ✅
- **Q3 Missing function** (the big one at scale):
  - The grid description still hardcodes «состояние **500 слов** вашей стартовой
    колоды» while I have **4000**. The memory map represents only 500/4000 (~12.5%)
    of my vocabulary — as a power user I **can't see most of my deck**, and there's
    no N3/N2/N1 breakdown, no full-deck heatmap, no level distribution. ⚠️⚠️
  - The stats header reads «0 слов использовано / 0 сессий завершено / 0 XP /
    Уровень погружения 0» despite a 4000-word, 900-due deck. Progress
    representation is **completely disconnected** from my actual mastery. ⚠️
    (Partly a seeding caveat for XP, but a 4000-word master showing "Level 0" is a
    glaring mismatch.)
- **Q4 Convenience**: The single resume CTA is fine, but the dashboard gives a power
  user **no at-a-glance control** (no "review N now", no level filter). ⚠️
- **Outcome**: advances. Console: 1 benign warning. Screenshot:
  [p3-01-home.png](screenshots/p3-01-home.png).

### 3.2 `/practice` (at scale)

- **Q1 Clarity**: «Активное повторение слов: У вас есть **900** слов готовых к
  повторению» with one button «🧠 Начать повторение [900]». «Всего неизученных слов:
  250», daily new «0 из 10». Readable. ✅
- **Q2 Next step**: Clear-ish, but see Q3.
- **Q3 Missing function** (power-user gaps):
  - **No session sizing / batching / filtering** for the 900-card review queue —
    one button dumps me into 900 with no "review 20", no "lapsed first", no level
    filter (N3/N2/N1). For a master this is the single biggest at-scale UX gap. ⚠️⚠️
  - The **«Баланс обучения (N5)»** widget is hardcoded to **N5** for an N3–N1
    master — it doesn't reflect my actual level. ⚠️
  - Daily-new limit is **10** with no visible way to raise the pace for an advanced
    learner who wants to push volume. ⚠️
- **Q4 Convenience**: The same dense dashboard as the beginners get — no power-user
  density/controls. The app treats a 4000-word master with the same chrome as a
  day-one user. ⚠️
- **Outcome**: advances. Screenshot: [p3-02-practice.png](screenshots/p3-02-practice.png).

### 3.3 Theme generation (Gemini) at scale

- **Q1 Clarity**: A proper loading state appears («Создание тем…» / «ИИ анализирует
  ваши слова и подбирает лучшие сценарии…»). ✅ (Better than I feared from P2.)
- **Q2 Next step**: Wait — but it **never finished**. `/api/gemini/classify` stayed
  in flight **>90s** and no scenarios appeared (the tab eventually went blank). For
  a large deck the pre-session word-classification appears to stall / not scale. ⚠️⚠️
- **Q3 Missing function**: No timeout/fallback/“this is taking long” messaging; no
  way to cap how many words feed the classifier. A power user with a big deck can't
  generate a chat session in reasonable time here.
- **Q4 Convenience**: Effectively **blocked at scale** — I could not reach a chat
  session as P3 within the time budget.
- **Outcome**: **blocked / errors** — session generation did not complete for a
  4000-word deck (`/api/gemini/classify` stalled). No console errors logged.

### 3.4 JLPT level selector (observed)

- The themes panel exposes a level selector where **N5 is active and N4–N1 are
  locked** (observed clearly in P2's generated themes; the same selector is present
  for P3). For a master with 4000 N3–N1 words, having only **N5 scenarios
  available** and N3–N2–N1 **locked** is a learning-logic mismatch — vocabulary
  scale does not unlock higher-level practice (levels appear gated on something
  else, e.g. grammar progress). ⚠️ (Recorded as observed; P3 couldn't exercise it
  further due to §3.3 + the purge.)

### 3.5 `/chat`, media, advanced practice

- Not reachable for P3 this run: chat needs a generated session (§3.3 stalled);
  media interactive playback is blocked (MeCab down, per P2 §3.5); and the
  synthetic deck was purged before further exploration. Recorded as
  **blocked** (services/seed limitation), not as passing.

## 5. Learning-journey verdict (in character)

"As an advanced learner, the app **doesn't scale to me**. The home screen proudly
said ‘900 to review’ but also ‘Level 0, 0 XP, 0 words used’ — it has no idea who I
am. My memory map only shows **500 of my 4000 words**, so I can't see the shape of
my real knowledge. When I went to review, I got **one button for 900 cards** — no
way to do 20, no way to focus on my weak N1 words or my lapses; that's not a tool
for a power user, it's a firehose. The **balance widget still says N5** as if I were
a beginner. And when I tried to start a conversation, the AI **never finished
analyzing my deck** — for a big vocabulary the session generator just hung. The
learning *philosophy* (FSRS, deck→chat→media loop) is sound and was lovely at the
beginner scale, but at 4000 words the app has **no level-awareness, no
session-sizing, and no power-user controls** — and higher-level scenarios are
locked behind something my vocabulary doesn't satisfy. It feels built for the first
500 words, not the next 3500."

## 6. Limitations / snapshot note

- **Synthetic seed + reconciliation caveat (important)**: P3's 4000 words were
  seeded into the local deck (`__local_starter__`). The app reconciles that category
  against the real 500-word starter deck on `/practice` load and **purged** the
  synthetic words mid-session (verified: `p3_master` IDB count returned to 0 after
  navigation). Valid at-scale observations (home «900», grid «Усвоенные», practice
  «900»/«N5», 250 new) were captured from the **initial seeded state**
  (screenshots p3-01, p3-02) before the purge. The purge itself is recorded as
  P3-H. Glosses were synthetic (reading-as-translation).
- **Theme generation** did not complete at scale (Gemini classify stalled >90s);
  chat/media/advanced-practice for P3 were therefore **blocked** and not exercised.
- Point-in-time snapshot; FSRS due counts and AI behavior vary by run. The earlier
  apparent "practice says 900 / quiz says all-reviewed" contradiction was an
  **artifact of the purge** (deck already emptied by the time the quiz was
  re-opened), not a genuine practice-vs-quiz defect — explicitly **not** reported as
  a bug.
